What each snapshot domain means:
- population: count, children, elderly, housing_capacity, homeless, idle_or_labourers, builders.
- food: stock_total, by_type, variety (food types in stock), consumption/production per month, months_of_food.
- winter: firewood, burn and production per month, months_of_firewood, clothing, shoes.
- storage: capacity, used, fill_ratio.
- production.staffing_lowest_first: buildings with the fewest workers relative to their maximum.
- farming: fields, field_tiles, avg_fertility; snapshot.fields holds per-field fertility, weeds, rocks, rotation.
- health, economy, defence, happiness: counts of services, soldiers, towers, walls, goods at zero.
Farming facts: fields 5x5 to 12x12, up to 7 farmers on 12x12; 3-year rotations; balance heavy feeders
(wheat) with nitrogen fixers (beans, clover); avoid the same crop in neighbouring fields (diseases).
